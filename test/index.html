1 · Work computer, once a day

One-time setup: show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B, or Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac), then drag this orange button onto it:

📥 Get DRH Inventory

Can't drag it? then right-click the bookmarks bar → Add page… and paste it as the URL.

Each morning: open homesales, log in, click Get DRH Inventory. A file named drh-inventory-DATE-TIME.json downloads.

Send that file to your work email or work OneDrive so you can open it on your phone.

2 · Phone or any device

Tap Load JSON and pick the newest file. It stays loaded on this device until you load a newer one, including with no signal.

iPhone home screen: in Safari, Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it from the icon and load the file there. The icon keeps its own copy, separate from Safari.
